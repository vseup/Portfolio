About Me

I am a software engineer with roots in design and a strong focus on human-computer interaction.

My path started in communication design, where I focused on digital interfaces and realized how much I enjoy building products, not just designing them. I then studied Media Informatics and Human-Computer Interaction at LMU Munich to combine creative problem-solving with a stronger technical foundation.

Today, I work as Engineer UX/UI Development at Torqeedo. I design and build interface concepts and software tools for hybrid marine propulsion systems. I bring a design-trained eye, a research mindset, and strong motivation to keep growing as an engineer.
